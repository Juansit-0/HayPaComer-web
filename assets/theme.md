# Pa' comer

Engraved kitchen theme from Pasto: cream paper and ink, flat chapters of Nariño produce, a heavy soft serif for the question "¿qué hay pa' comer?", and numbers as the largest thing on every screen.

## Color Palette

- **Papel**: `#f3ecdf` - Ground
- **Carta**: `#fbf6ec` - Cards, fields and chips on the paper
- **Tinta**: `#1c1712` - Text, borders and hard shadows
- **Papa amarilla**: `#f3c443` - Chapter Ahora; at-risk food
- **Tomate de árbol**: `#c8432a` - Chapter Mercado; expired, door open
- **Cilantro**: `#2f7a4a` - Chapter Nevera; fresh and done
- **Mora**: `#4b1f2e` - Chapter Números; under review
- **Cobre**: `#a8481f` - Primary action, chapter Chef

## Typography

- **Display**: Fraunces 900 (soft, slightly wonky, heaviest optical size) - wordmark, headlines, loader, uppercase
- **Text and data**: Hanken Grotesk - reading, controls, tabular numbers

## Best Used For

Food and kitchen products with real data, household apps, hardware with a companion app, landing pages that sell with one big question and one big number.

## Notes

- One chapter colour per screen, at full strength; no tints or gradients.
- Cards are edged in ink with a hard offset shadow; lists are rows on the paper.
- Text on tomate, cilantro and mora is arroz; text on papa and paper is tinta.
- Motion explains change and disappears under reduced motion.
